Configuration
=============

The same company settings are available from:

* Accounting → Configuration → Settings
* Settings → Companies

Receivable Approval
-------------------

Enable this to require approval whenever a partner receivable account changes.

Payable Approval
----------------

Enable this to require approval whenever a partner payable account changes.

Approval Category
-----------------

Choose the standard Approvals category used for generated requests.

Blocking Scope
--------------

**Pending account only**
    Block posting only when the exact pending account is used.

**All partner accounts**
    Block all journal items on the relevant receivable/payable side while approval
    is pending.
