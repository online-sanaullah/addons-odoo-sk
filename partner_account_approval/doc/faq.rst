FAQ
===

Why can a draft be saved?
-------------------------

The module intentionally blocks posting rather than draft preparation.

Why is cancellation hidden?
----------------------------

No previously approved account exists for rollback.

Where is approval history?
--------------------------

Open the partner and click **Account History**.

Why can another account still be posted?
----------------------------------------

The company may be configured to block only the exact pending account. Select
**Block All Receivable/Payable Accounts for the Partner** for stricter control.
