Usage
=====

Account Change
--------------

Change the receivable or payable account on the partner and save. The approval
request is created immediately.

Draft Transactions
------------------

Invoices, bills, payments and journal entries can be saved in draft.

Posting
-------

Posting is blocked while approval is pending, according to the company blocking
scope.

Cancellation
------------

Cancellation is available only when an approved historical account exists for
rollback.
